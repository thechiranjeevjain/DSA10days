## Read this first — what the order does and does not do

**The 263-row ledger and the linked-prompt table are DSA only.** Their purpose is to notice which statements and pattern triggers remain accessible. The **whole daily card** is mixed: two DSA implementation moves, **five core DSA statement cues plus up to five optional**, one LLD, one HLD, and two Java/backend questions. Friday's mock samples more of the actual interview format. Company sheets, SQL, projects, résumé stories, and courses are routed by the next mock or booked round; they are not all silently included in the 263.

**The order meets diversity and coverage requirements at cue level, not mastery or unique-value requirements.** It offers every unique linked DSA statement in each 27-day pass, spreads 22 named families, and avoids adjacent same-family prompts in a generated pass. By making the first half of each row core on one pass and the other half core on the next, it gives every linked prompt a **core cue within 54 days** if no days are missed. A different URL or broad family label does not prove a distinct code move or fill *your* gap; roughly 90 prior attempts still lack title-level scores. The coach currently elevates recorded red/yellow full attempts and some lost once-green design/code cues. It does **not yet** automatically put every DSA inventory `Y/U`, Java `Y/U`, or mock gap into a dated review slot. Use the bounded Sunday repair decision below for those gaps.

**Five core cues are the rational default; ten are a ceiling.** Give each statement **30–45 seconds** for the likely pattern and first invariant; mark `U` and move on when the move is not acquired. Five take roughly **3–4 minutes of thinking plus opening/scoring time**. Do any of the five optional prompts only if the entire inventory block remains within **10 minutes** and attention is still good. Mark optional prompts `S` (skipped) rather than pretending they were recalled. The other lanes keep the daily card at about **20–30 minutes**. Ten *full* DSA solves daily would require hours and displace design, mocks, applications, work, and recovery. On an interview day, the call override replaces the card; after three bad days, follow the seven-day lighter protocol. Missing a card creates no evening catch-up debt.

**Source-to-practice rule:** one canonical scored item per distinct move. A company sheet or course page is used after a cold attempt or for a confirmed company format. If it reveals a genuinely new, repeatedly relevant move, register that move in the owning DSA/LLD/HLD/Java deck and spend an existing deep slot on first acquisition. Do not add all pages of the source to the daily queue. The selected daily decks are **263 DSA prompts, 15 LLD, 8 HLD, and 20 Java/backend questions**, plus mocks; they are not a claim that all files in every folder have been learned.

**The actual operating algorithm, in order:**

1. If an interview is booked within 72 hours, use the [matching interview override](SEP_20_30_2026_INTERVIEW_READY_HOURLY_WINS.md#interview-override--exact-replacement-no-added-hours); within 24 hours, use its short version and protect sleep. This replaces normal work.
2. If three days have been unproductive, use the [seven-day lighter protocol](SEP_20_30_2026_INTERVIEW_READY_HOURLY_WINS.md#rejection-recovery--prevent-another-ten-day-stall). Resume at the current calendar card afterward; do not make up skipped rows.
3. Otherwise, run `G:\TechStudyNotes\review-os\coach\coach.cmd plan`. Perform its mixed cues within 20–30 minutes and stop. `G` is an independent cue, `Y` partial/slow, `U` not acquired, and `S` a skipped optional inventory cue; none is a full cold solve.
4. In a scheduled deep block, select the confirmed-round gap first, then the most consequential actual/mock failure or due red/yellow, then the next numbered DSA/LLD/HLD item. A repair **replaces** the numbered item; the displaced cursor stays next. Score a full attempt only after code/design and tests or changed-requirement explanation.
5. On Sunday, compare full attempts and mocks against cue scores and energy. Take at most **one new repair per domain** into the following week's existing slots. A repeated inventory `U` is an acquisition candidate, not an urgent daily review. If recognition is green but cold performance is weak, use **only the five core cues** and put any remaining time into reconstruction.

This is an **adaptive weekly decision rule around a deterministic daily circuit**. The current software automates the circuit and some full-attempt repairs. It does not yet implement every Sunday judgement or an adaptive spacing model for all inventory and Java cues; do not infer those features from the generated card.

**Total:** approximately **20–30 minutes** for the entire daily cue sweep, depending on optional inventory prompts. A full timed attempt that happens to be today's assigned item satisfies that item's cue; spend only the residual time on the other lanes. The deep blocks, mock, and job actions already in the hourly plan retain their stop times. On office days do the sweep in the 07:15–07:45 window. On WFH/weekend days distribute the cues inside the existing feedback/weekly-reset minutes; do not add a night shift. If a real interview or exhaustion replaces a day, mark it skipped and resume with the current date's card; no catch-up stack.

**Why this format:** retrieval, spacing, and interleaving can improve later performance, and retrieval can transfer to changed questions ([Sana and Yan, 2022](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/09567976211057507/); [Pan and Rickard, 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29733621/)). Those studies do **not** establish that this particular number of Java problems, cues, or days guarantees recall. Cold mocks and your own score trend decide whether the cadence works.

## Fixed round-robin counters — no daily topic choice

The automatic coach owns the calendar counters; there is no morning topic choice. `coach.cmd plan` chooses two A/S code moves, five core and up to five optional inventory prompts, one LLD, one HLD, and two Java questions beginning **5 October**. The loops are 16 days for DSA styles, **54 days for every inventory prompt's core turn**, 15 for LLD, 8 for HLD, and 10 for Java. A full solve or mock counts for an assigned cue only if it covered that exact item. If the inventory timer stops after the five core prompts, enter `S` for the optional five in `record-day`; if a core prompt was not attempted, leave the day's formal report missing and note what happened. Never record unseen prompts as green. On a missed day, resume with the current date's card. There is no backlog or catch-up quota.

For each attempted cue, write `G` (independent), `Y` (slow/partial), or `U` (never acquired). For the inventory row, enter one ten-character result string in the coach's printed order, using `S` for optional prompts not attempted (for example `GGGUGSSSSS`); on the three-prompt short row score all three. Use a dated note for the other lanes. `Y`/`U` does **not** force a same-day full solve. On Sunday, choose the highest-impact **one** weak style for the next scheduled deep block: first an upcoming interview/mock failure, then a coding invariant needed across variants, then a design state/failure gap, then Java. Break ties by earliest numbered cue. Keep the remaining weak cues in the ledger/scorecard; when their round-robin turn recurs, check again. A failed daily cue that also blocks the next mock may replace a full anchor sooner. The daily sweep must stay short.

**Mixing rule:** the independent 16-, 54-, 15-, 8-, and 10-day core cycles produce a changing combination each day. DSA inventory uses the 22 pattern families in the [source index](07_LEETCODE_SOLVED_INDEX.md), plus the classified v15 extras: each 27-day offered pass contains every unique URL once, no two adjacent prompts in a generated pass use the same family, and no 10-prompt row contains more than two from one family. Core and optional halves swap on the second pass; after both passes, within-family order changes. This is a scheduling rule, not proof that two prompts with different labels exercise independent skills. Mock failures and recorded red/yellow full attempts take the next relevant deep slot.

**Calibrate on 1 November:** Count `G/Y/U/S` separately for (a) cues, (b) full timed DSA, (c) LLD/HLD, and (d) mock outcomes. If cue `G` is high but full-code/mock performance is weak, stay at five core/day and use spare minutes for targeted cold reconstruction. If full performance is strong and energy stable, optional cues can be done within the same 10-minute cap. Never use a quick cue as proof of interview readiness.

## One-day interview-call refresh

When a call arrives, replace the next **two-hour** block: **10 minutes** confirm role/round and format; **60 minutes** matching mock from blank; **25 minutes** feedback and one highest-impact repair; **15 minutes** role-specific story or design; **10 minutes** logistics. Stop. A weakness revealed by this test becomes the next scheduled repair. If the round is within 24 hours, use the main plan's shorter under-24-hour override and protect sleep.
